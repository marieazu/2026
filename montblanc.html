Montblanc

Elevating E-commerce Within Platform Constraints

Responsive Web  •  Luxury

Context

Montblanc partnered with YOOX Net-A-Porter to scale their e-commerce infrastructure. While this provided a robust foundation, it also imposed strict CMS templates and technical boundaries. The challenge was to create a distinctly Montblanc experience without compromising the integrity of the shared platform.

Core Challenge

Our objective was to deliver a premium, bespoke brand experience within a standardised e-commerce framework designed for high-volume retail. We had to find the "cracks" in the system where we could inject luxury craft without breaking the underlying technical rules.

Strategic Approach

We operated in close collaboration with Montblanc stakeholders and YOOX engineers to test ideas against platform constraints early. Rather than fighting the system, we focused on High-Finish execution in the areas where flexibility existed, ensuring every design choice was technically viable from the outset.

1. Progressive Navigation Strategy

Encouraging discovery within a rigid framework

Users required a clearer sense of Montblanc's full product range, but the default navigation patterns were limiting exploration and felt disconnected from the brand's premium positioning.

Solution:

We introduced a side-panel navigation that expanded progressively, making better use of screen real estate while remaining fully compliant with YOOX guidelines. We refined the micro-interactions in collaboration with engineering, testing hover and click behaviours to ensure a seamless, tactile feel that mirrored the brand's physical product interactions.

2. Digital Craft and Materiality

Translating the in-store experience to a digital template

Luxury products rely heavily on a sense of materiality and detail. Communicating the weight and craftsmanship of a Montblanc pen or watch is inherently difficult within static, standardized templates.

Solution:

We prioritised rich product imagery and immersive content, integrating video and 3D elements where the platform supported them. By focusing on Visual Hierarchy and high-fidelity assets, we bridged the gap between physical and digital browsing, allowing the craftsmanship of the products to lead the experience.

3. Bespoke Expression Within a Shared CMS

Defining brand character through refined interaction

The primary risk was that the site would feel like a generic template rather than a luxury destination. We needed to differentiate the interface while operating within standardised CMS components.

Solution:

Through deep collaboration with developers, we identified "edge cases" where the CMS could support more advanced styling. We applied refined spacing, crafted transitions, and subtle motion to create a bespoke feel. These interventions allowed the brand to stand out without sacrificing the performance or maintainability of the shared platform.

Outcome

The result was a refined, durable e-commerce experience that respected technical boundaries while elevating Montblanc's digital presence.

Strategic Balance: Proved that strong brand expression is possible within highly structured enterprise systems.

Improved Discovery: Enhanced the navigation model to support a wider range of product categories.

Consistency: Delivered a world-class experience that remained stable and performant across all devices.
