SYKY

MVP Launch for a Luxury Web3 Fashion Marketplace

Responsive Web  •  Web3  •  Fashion

Context

SYKY is a community-driven platform where digital fashion designers release limited-edition collections and collectors showcase NFT wardrobes. With London Fashion Week only three months away, the team required an MVP that supported discovery, high-value purchases, and community building.

Core Challenge

The goal was to design a premium, coherent marketplace experience for both creators and collectors within a compressed timeline. We had to balance the highly expressive needs of individual fashion houses with a stable, scalable engineering framework.

Strategic Approach

We focused on the high-finish delivery of the core user journey: browsing exclusive drops, managing auctions, and curating personal galleries. This required distilling fragmented brand assets into a single, intentional visual system that could handle the complexity of Web3 onboarding without losing its luxury appeal.

1. Systematic Customisation

Balancing creative expression with platform consistency

Fashion designers required their drops to feel bespoke, yet engineering required a reusable framework.

Solution: We developed a layout strategy that used asset-driven differentiation. By creating a rigid grid structure but allowing for varied artwork scales, imagery formats, and motion treatments, each collection felt unique while the codebase remained stable.

To reinforce the brand identity, we integrated the SYKY "corridor" motif as subtle, abstract motion backgrounds. This added depth and atmosphere to the UI without compromising legibility or site performance.

2. Streamlining the Purchase Journey

Reducing friction in high-value transactions

Web3 complexity, specifically wallet setup, was a significant barrier. Any disruption in the flow led to immediate drop-off.

Solution: We designed an integrated purchase flow using MoonPay to support fiat payments directly within the interface. By containing the journey within the SYKY environment, we maintained a seamless, premium experience that removed the technical hurdles of traditional crypto onboarding.

3. Identity and Community

Transforming a marketplace into a social gallery

For digital fashion, ownership is about visibility. Users needed a way to express their identity beyond a simple transaction history.

Solution: We designed member profiles as personal NFT galleries, allowing collectors to curate their wardrobes and follow other users. This positioned the platform as a social space for fashion enthusiasts, shifting the focus from one-off purchases to long-term community engagement.

4. Design System Architecture

Establishing visual coherence under pressure

Fragmented work from external agencies and internal teams had led to growing visual inconsistency just weeks before launch.

Solution: We audited the existing design landscape to isolate a core visual language. We removed redundant styles and established a unified component system in Figma. This allowed the team to move at speed during the final sprint while ensuring every screen met the high-fidelity standards required for a London Fashion Week debut.

Impact

The MVP launched successfully to coincide with London Fashion Week.

Market Validation: Items from the debut collection sold out within 48 hours.

Auction Engagement: The primary auction piece saw consistent bidding until the final hour.

Scalability: The design system provided a stable foundation for post-launch feature expansion.
